# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We, the members, contributors and maintainers of kit, pledge to make
participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.
This holds regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that help an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps our community:

- Show empathy and kindness to other people.
- Respect different opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give constructive feedback, and accept it with grace.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize to the people they affect and
  learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community as a whole, not only for you.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Use sexualized language or imagery, or make sexual advances of any kind.
- Troll, insult or make derogatory comments, or attack a person personally or
  politically.
- Harass a person in public or in private.
- Publish the private information of others, such as a physical or email
  address, without their explicit permission.
- Do other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders explain and enforce our standards of acceptable behavior.
They act fairly and appropriately in response to any behavior that they judge
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues and other contributions that do
not follow this code of conduct. They communicate the reasons for a
moderation decision when appropriate.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all community spaces. It also applies when a
person officially represents the community in public spaces. Examples include
use of an official email address, a post through an official social media
account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline
event.

The community spaces of kit include the repository, its issues, its pull
requests and its discussions.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior to the
maintainers listed in [`MAINTAINERS.md`](MAINTAINERS.md). Do not report it in
a public issue.

kit has no email address for conduct reports. The only private channel is the
one in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Open a private report through GitHub
Security Advisories at
<https://github.com/officialunofficial/kit/security/advisories/new>. Start the
title with `CONDUCT`. State that the report is about conduct and not about a
vulnerability.

The maintainers review and investigate every report promptly and fairly. They
respect the privacy and security of the person who reports an incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders follow these guidelines when they decide the consequences
of an action that breaks this code of conduct.

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behavior that the
community finds unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** A private, written warning from community leaders. The
warning explains the nature of the violation and why the behavior was
inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:** A violation through a single incident or a series of
actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. The
person has no interaction with the people involved for a set period. This
includes unsolicited interaction with the people who enforce this code. It
also includes interaction in community spaces and on external channels such
as social media. A breach of these terms can lead to a temporary or permanent
ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Community impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including
sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from any interaction or public communication
with the community for a set period. During this period, the person has no
public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited
interaction with the people who enforce this code. A breach of these terms can
lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Community impact:** A pattern of violation of community standards. This
includes sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of a person, or
aggression toward or disparagement of classes of people.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from any public interaction within the
community.
